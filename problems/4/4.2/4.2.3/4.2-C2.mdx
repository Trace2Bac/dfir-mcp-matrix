## 현상과 피해

상한 안에서 센 건수나 찾은 것이 없다는 결과가 대상 전체의 결과처럼 전달되고, 앞 10 MiB의 해시가 이미지 해시로 옮겨질 수 있다.

### [Wireshark-MCP](/problems/servers/Wireshark-MCP)

- 반환량처럼 보이는 `limit`을 tshark `-c`로 넘겨 표시 필터에 맞지 않는 패킷까지 세므로, 도구는 앞 `limit`개 패킷 안에서만 필터에 맞는 것을 찾는다. 300개 패킷 파일에 `frame.number > 150`을 주자 `wireshark_read_packets`·`wireshark_decode_as`·`wireshark_set_protocol_prefs`가 기본 `limit`에서 빈 배열이나 머리글만 success로 돌려줬고, `wireshark_decrypt_tls`는 100으로 고정해 뒤쪽 HTTP를 싣지 않았다. 응답에 확인 범위 표시가 없다. 코드: [src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_packets.py:25-31](https://github.com/bx33661/Wireshark-MCP/blob/0e1b6595a2dcfe097133deb1c0c3128fb7a110d4/src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_packets.py#L25-L31); [src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_extraction.py:227-273](https://github.com/bx33661/Wireshark-MCP/blob/0e1b6595a2dcfe097133deb1c0c3128fb7a110d4/src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_extraction.py#L227-L273), [src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_extraction.py:322-325](https://github.com/bx33661/Wireshark-MCP/blob/0e1b6595a2dcfe097133deb1c0c3128fb7a110d4/src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_extraction.py#L322-L325), [src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_extraction.py:357-360](https://github.com/bx33661/Wireshark-MCP/blob/0e1b6595a2dcfe097133deb1c0c3128fb7a110d4/src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_extraction.py#L357-L360), [src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_extraction.py:392-395](https://github.com/bx33661/Wireshark-MCP/blob/0e1b6595a2dcfe097133deb1c0c3128fb7a110d4/src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_extraction.py#L392-L395), [src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_extraction.py:431-434](https://github.com/bx33661/Wireshark-MCP/blob/0e1b6595a2dcfe097133deb1c0c3128fb7a110d4/src/wireshark_mcp/tshark/_extraction.py#L431-L434); [src/wireshark_mcp/tools/advanced.py:41-52](https://github.com/bx33661/Wireshark-MCP/blob/0e1b6595a2dcfe097133deb1c0c3128fb7a110d4/src/wireshark_mcp/tools/advanced.py#L41-L52)
- `wireshark_scan_file_signatures`는 하위 추출 `search_packet_contents(..., limit=50)`의 결과를 세면서 `truncated`를 읽지 않아 상한에 닿으면 탐지 수가 50으로 고정되고, 응답은 `PNG: 50 packet(s)`를 전체 탐지 수처럼 싣는다(PNG 서명 패킷 80개 캡처). 코드: [src/wireshark_mcp/tools/forensics.py:112-148](https://github.com/bx33661/Wireshark-MCP/blob/0e1b6595a2dcfe097133deb1c0c3128fb7a110d4/src/wireshark_mcp/tools/forensics.py#L112-L148); [src/wireshark_mcp/tools/protocol.py:84-96](https://github.com/bx33661/Wireshark-MCP/blob/0e1b6595a2dcfe097133deb1c0c3128fb7a110d4/src/wireshark_mcp/tools/protocol.py#L84-L96)
